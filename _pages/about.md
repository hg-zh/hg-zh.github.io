I am an Assistant Professor in [STOR](https://stor.unc.edu) and [SDIS](https://sdis.unc.edu) at UNC-Chapel Hill. Before joining UNC, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Statistics at UC Davis, working with Professors [Hans-Georg Müller](https://anson.ucdavis.edu/~mueller/) and [Jane-Ling Wang](https://anson.ucdavis.edu/~wang/). I got my Ph.D. in Statistics from Peking University in 2022, under the supervision of Professor [Fang Yao](https://www.math.pku.edu.cn/teachers/yaof/Homepage.html).

## My current research interests include:


- Statistical modeling and inference for object data, including but not limited to distributions, trees, and compositional data
- Functional data with complex structure, including discretely observed and non-Euclidean data
- Learning theory, generalization bound for deep neural network
- Uncertainty quantification, generative models and their applications
